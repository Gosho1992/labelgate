A supplier changed an ingredient. Which of your product labels are now wrong?

LabelGate reads the supplier's document with AI on this laptop — nothing is uploaded — re-checks every affected label in milliseconds, and makes a person confirm before anything is printed.

Guided, one step at a time · synthetic example data
Private — supplier specs, label artwork, formulas: read by Gemini Nano on this device (Firebase AI Logic ONLY_ON_DEVICE). Checked by fixed rules in this browser. Never uploaded.
Public — recall notices: hybrid (Firebase AI Logic PREFER_ON_DEVICE): Gemini Nano on this device when available, otherwise Gemini in the cloud (App Check). Then matched to your products locally.
How it works — the steps in writing, and requirements
  1. Gate 1: click Use demo document (a photographed Spanish supplier spec — synthetic). Gemini Nano reads it on this device; the text appears as it is read (30–90 s on a laptop without a GPU).
  2. Check the reading against the image, correct anything wrong, and click Confirm this reading. You see what changed — and that milk is now an allergen source.
  3. Every product using this ingredient is checked instantly against its verified label record: which labels are now wrong, and why.
  4. Gate 2: on a blocked product, click Check new artwork →, use the demo artwork (the corrected label), confirm, and sign it off for print.

No on-device AI in your browser? Use Type it myself (no AI) to try the rule checks — this is clearly marked and is not on-device inference. Requirements for on-device AI: Chrome 148+ on desktop, 16 GB RAM or a GPU with more than 4 GB VRAM, 22 GB free disk. The public cloud half of the hybrid design is in the Recall notices tab.
